# Isolator context

Project terminology is maintained in [GLOSSARY.md](./GLOSSARY.md).

Isolator is a self-hosted, multi-tenant ISMS for mid-sized businesses in Germany going for their first ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certificate and keeping it afterwards. It is MIT licensed, runs without any required outside service, and its interface is available in German, English and French.

The product follows the ISMS lifecycle:

1. **Establish** – define scope, decide which controls apply (SoA), set risk criteria, build the asset and supplier registers, run the first risk assessment.
2. **Operate** – work through tasks, collect evidence, handle incidents.
3. **Review** – recurring risk assessments, internal audits, management review, nonconformities.
4. **Certify** – the readiness checklist and an audit file export for the certification body.

Every decision on an SoA entry, a risk rating, a risk treatment or a Document version is a Confirmation by a named Member. Every step works by hand. Automated scans and AI come later, and will only ever produce drafts and evidence: AI assists but never decides.
