# Glossary

**Organization** – a tenant; the business running an ISMS on Isolator.

**ISMS** – Information Security Management System: the policies, processes and controls an organization operates to manage information security (ISO 27001 clauses 4–10).

**Scope** – the boundaries of the ISMS (clause 4.3): which locations, units, systems are covered.

**Control** – an entry in a control catalog, e.g. Annex A 2022 `A.8.13 Information backup`. Catalog controls are global and read-only.

**Statement of Applicability (SoA)** – per organization, one entry per catalog control stating whether it applies, why, and its implementation status.

**Asset** – something of value to the organization that needs protecting: information, software, hardware, services, people, sites.

**Risk** – a potential event that harms an asset's confidentiality, integrity or availability. Scored as likelihood × impact (1–5 each).

**Risk treatment** – the decision for a risk: mitigate (apply controls), accept, transfer, or avoid.

**Risk assessment** – a dated review of the risk register. The first one establishes the register; recurring ones (at least yearly, or on significant change) revisit it.

**Scan** – an automated check against an organization's systems (cloud accounts, identity provider, endpoints, external surface) that produces findings.

**Finding** – a single result of a scan, which may raise or update a risk or count as evidence for a control.

**Evidence** – an artifact demonstrating a control is operating (document, screenshot, scan result, log export).
